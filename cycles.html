Does this deal leave a profit?

Contribution after the costs entered, before tax, unallocated overhead and your time. Unsold stock and further repairs can reduce it. This is not guaranteed profit.

How the estimate is built

Every adjustment is visible. These depreciation and condition factors are our starting assumptions, not observed IIT Bombay transaction data.

V = new price × age retention × cosmetic condition × size demand − outstanding repairs, rounded down to ₹50.
Buy only if quote < 0.60 × V. Sell target = 0.80 × V.

Price reference & assumptions

Decathlon · B’Twin My Bike: ₹6,999 retail selling price observed 1 November 2026; steel, single-speed, V-brakes. Verify price, size and availability before a real deal.

Mumbai second-hand listings on OLX provide asking-price context only. Different Rockrider models are not direct My Bike comparables; no OLX price is inserted into this formula.

Brand and component quality enter through your matched new-bike price. Size changes resale liquidity only if the buyer pool is limited; frame size and wheel diameter are different measurements. The ±20% range is a sensitivity band, not a statistical confidence interval. No live scraping or claim of campus sales calibration.

This local prototype covers conventional adult bicycles up to ₹1 lakh new. E-bikes, carbon frames and collector bicycles need specialist appraisal. Inspection requests and owner-added stock are saved locally. No payments, automatic appointments or purchases are connected.
